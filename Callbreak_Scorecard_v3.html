Callbreak Pro

Ye ek digital scorecard aur point calculator hai. Pen-paper ki zaroorat nahi: 4 players ki bid aur hath (tricks) daalo, score apne aap calculate ho jayega.

Features

  • Har round ki bid, trump (hukam) aur made hands track hote hain
  • Dealer aur Cut (agla dealer) har round automatically rotate hota hai
  • Undo: pichla data wapas aa jata hai, bina dobara likhe edit kar sakte ho
  • Game timer: match kitni der se chal raha hai
  • Last player ka auto-fill aur 13 hath ka live tally
  • Scoreboard ko image mein save ya share karo
  • Game apne aap save hota hai, page refresh karne par bhi wahin se continue
  • 14 themes (Dark, Light, Gemini, Sky Blue + 10 aur) aur Auto mode, sound on/off
  • Timer par tap karke pause / resume; History mein purane games aur wins leaderboard
  • Game ke end par highlights (sharpshooter, best round, roughest round)
  • Bid / made box mein Enter dabao to agle box par chale jao

Aapka data sirf aapke device ke browser mein save hota hai.

Basics

  • 4 players, 52 patte (joker nahi). Har player ko 13 patte.
  • Jo shuffle karta hai wo dealer hai. Cut karne wala player agla dealer banta hai.
  • Ek game mein 5 se 8 rounds (setup mein choose karo).

Bidding

Patte dekhkar har player bolta hai ki wo kitne hath jeetega: kam se kam 1, zyada se zyada 13. Is app mein har round ka trump (hukam) select kar sakte ho. Traditional Callbreak mein ♠ Spades hamesha trump hota hai.

Khelna

  • Pehla player koi bhi patta chalta hai.
  • Baaki players ko same suit ka patta dena hota hai, ho sake to bada patta.
  • Suit nahi hai to trump se kaat sakte ho.
  • Sabse bada trump jeetta hai; trump na ho to lead suit ka sabse bada patta.
  • Hath jeetne wala agla patta chalta hai.
  • Round ke end mein sabke hath ka total exactly 13 hona chahiye.

Scoring (is scorecard mein)

  • Bid pura ya thoda zyada (max +2 extra): score = bid × 10 + extra
  • Bid se kam hath: score = −(bid × 10)
  • 3 ya zyada extra hath: bhi −(bid × 10) (is scorecard ka rule)
Bid 3, made 3 → 30
Bid 3, made 4 → 31 (3.1 points)
Bid 4, made 3 → −40
Bid 2, made 5 → −20

Winner

Saare rounds ke baad jiska total score sabse zyada ho, wahi winner hai. 👑
